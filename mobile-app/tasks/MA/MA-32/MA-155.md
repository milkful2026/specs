## 2. Problem Statement

Customers can see their upcoming deliveries but can't cancel them in the app. MA-32 needs:
- a cancel action for both kinds of upcoming delivery, before the 20:00 IST cut-off;
- the cancellation policy shown up front, with an optional reason;
- a confirmation of what was refunded;
- a clear block after the cut-off.

The app also mislabels the result today. `isKnownNotCharged` treats **every** `CANCELLED` order
as "not charged", but a customer cancel is charged and then refunded.

## 3. Scope

### In scope

- **Order Detail:** a **Cancel order** action for a cancellable order, with a policy and reason
  sheet, confirmation, and a result state.
- **Scheduled Delivery:** a **Cancel delivery** action that calls the existing
  `SubscriptionRepository.skip`.
- Post-cut-off messaging on both screens.
- Copy and status handling for `CUSTOMER_CANCELLED` orders, including "Refund in progress".
- A **Shop for tomorrow** link after a successful cancel, which goes to `/catalog`.
- Model and repository additions for MA-154's API and DTO fields.

### Out of scope

- Push/SMS notifications (MA-103).
- Editing an order in place.
- Cancelling after the cut-off, or orders that aren't `CONFIRMED` (MA-154 rejects them; the app
  never offers the action).
- Cancel from the My Orders list rows. The action lives on the detail screens only; the list
  just reflects the new status.

## 4. Functional Requirements

### FR-1: When "Cancel order" shows (Order Detail)

- It shows only in `OrderDetailLoaded`, when `order.cancellableUntil != null` **and**
  `now < cancellableUntil` (`now` from the injected clock).
- `cancellableUntil` comes from the server (MA-154 FR-7). The app never computes the cut-off
  itself.
- **Placement and style:**
  - a `TextButton` labelled **Cancel order**, `Key('orderDetail.cancel')`, under MA-152's
    Reorder/Support row;
  - error-coloured text (`colorScheme.error`), at least 48 dp tall;
  - preceded by the policy line `Text('Free cancellation until {h:mm a}, {EEE d MMM}')`, which
    is `cancellableUntil` in IST (e.g. "Free cancellation until 8:00 PM, Wed 7 Oct").

### FR-2: Cancel sheet (Order Detail)

Tapping **Cancel order** opens a modal bottom sheet, `Key('cancelOrder.sheet')`:

- **Title:** **Cancel this order?**
- **Policy:** "You'll get a full refund of ₹{amount} to your Milkful Wallet." For a ₹0 order:
  "Nothing was charged for this order."
- **Reason** (optional): a label "Reason (optional)" and four `ChoiceChip`s with keys
  `cancelOrder.reason.{wire}`:
  - "Ordered by mistake" (`ORDERED_BY_MISTAKE`)
  - "Won't be home" (`NOT_HOME`)
  - "Changed my mind" (`CHANGED_MIND`)
  - "Other" (`OTHER`)

  No chip is selected by default. Tapping a selected chip deselects it.
- **Buttons:**
  - **Keep order** (`OutlinedButton`, `Key('cancelOrder.keep')`) closes the sheet. Nothing is
    sent.
  - **Cancel order** (`FilledButton`, error colour, `Key('cancelOrder.confirm')`).
- **While the request runs:**
  - the confirm button shows a spinner in place of its label and is disabled;
  - Keep order is disabled;
  - the sheet can't be dismissed.

### FR-3: Result (Order Detail)

`OrderRepository.cancel(orderId, reason)` → `POST /orders/{orderId}/cancel`.

- **Success:**
  - The sheet closes and the screen redraws from the returned order (no extra GET).
  - SnackBar:
    - `refundState == REFUNDED`: "Order cancelled. ₹{amount} refunded to your Wallet."
    - `PENDING`: "Order cancelled. Your refund is on its way."
    - `NOT_REQUIRED`: "Order cancelled."
  - The SnackBar action is **Shop for tomorrow** → `context.go('/catalog')`.
- **`409 CUTOFF_PASSED`:**
  - The sheet closes and the order is reloaded.
  - SnackBar: "It's past the 8 PM cut-off, so this order can't be cancelled now."
  - The Cancel action disappears, because the reloaded `cancellableUntil` is null.
- **`409 ORDER_NOT_CANCELLABLE`:** the sheet closes, the order is reloaded, and the SnackBar
  reads "This order can't be cancelled anymore."
- **Network or other error:** the sheet **stays open** with an inline error "Couldn't cancel.
  Try again.", and the confirm button is re-enabled. No state is lost and the chosen reason is
  kept.

### FR-4: Cancelled-order display (all order screens)

`order_status_copy.dart`:

- **`isKnownNotCharged`:** `CANCELLED` is "not charged" **only when** `failureReason !=
  'CUSTOMER_CANCELLED'`. A customer cancel was charged and refunded, so the amount is **not**
  struck through.
- **`reasonText('CUSTOMER_CANCELLED')`** depends on `refundState`:
  - `REFUNDED` → "You cancelled this order. ₹{amount} was refunded to your Wallet."
  - `PENDING` → "You cancelled this order. Your refund is in progress."
  - `NOT_REQUIRED` or null → "You cancelled this order."

  `reasonText` gains an optional `OrderSummary` parameter, so it can read the amount and
  `refundState`.
- **`billCaption`** for a customer-cancelled order: "Refunded to Wallet", or "Refund in
  progress" while `PENDING`.
- **Status chip:** unchanged ("Cancelled").

### FR-5: Cancel delivery (Scheduled Delivery)

On `ScheduledDeliveryScreen` (a subscription's next delivery, not yet an order):

- **When it shows:** a **Cancel delivery** `TextButton`, `Key('scheduled.cancel')`, with the
  same style as FR-1. It shows only while `now < cutoff(entry.date)`.
  - `cutoff(entry.date)` is 20:00 IST on `entry.date - 1`. It uses the existing
    `ist_clock.dart` helpers; this mirrors Subscription Service, which is authoritative.
  - Policy line: "Free cancellation until 8:00 PM, {EEE d MMM}".
- **Confirm dialog:** `AlertDialog`, `Key('cancelDelivery.dialog')`.
  - Title: **Cancel this delivery?**
  - Body: "Your {product} delivery on {EEE d MMM} won't be sent and you won't be charged. Your
    subscription continues as usual."
  - Buttons: **Keep delivery** / **Cancel delivery** (`Key('cancelDelivery.confirm')`).
  - No reason picker: Skip has no reason field, and nothing is charged.
- **On confirm:** call `SubscriptionRepository.skip(subscriptionId, entry.date)`.
  - **Success:**
    - SnackBar "Delivery on {EEE d MMM} cancelled." with a **Shop for tomorrow** action →
      `/catalog`;
    - then `context.pop()` back to My Orders, which refreshes and no longer lists that date.
  - **`CUTOFF_PASSED`** (Subscription Service; also "already processed") → SnackBar "It's
    past the 8 PM cut-off, so this delivery can't be cancelled now." Then the cubit's existing
    refresh runs. If the delivery has already become an order, that path shows it with the
    `becameOrder` flow (MA-146), where FR-1 applies if it's still cancellable.
  - **Network or other error:** SnackBar "Couldn't cancel. Try again." and the screen is
    unchanged.

### FR-6: Past the cut-off

- **Order Detail:** when the order is `CONFIRMED` but `cancellableUntil` is null or past, show
  the muted line "Cancellation closed at 8 PM the day before delivery." No button.
- **Scheduled Delivery:** the same line once `now >= cutoff`.

### FR-7: Data layer

- **`OrderSummary`** gains `cancellableUntil` (`DateTime?`), `cancelReason` (`CancelReason?`),
  `cancelledAt` (`DateTime?`) and `refundState` (`RefundState?`), parsed tolerantly: an
  unknown or missing value → null.
- **`OrderRepository.cancel(String orderId, {CancelReason? reason}) -> Future<OrderSummary>`**
  - `DioOrderRepository`: `POST ${AppConfig.orderBaseUrl}/orders/$orderId/cancel`, with the
    body `{"reason": wire}` or `{}`.
  - Errors surface as `ApiException`. The cubit branches on `errorCode`.
- **`FakeOrderRepository`:** `cancelResult`, `cancelException`, a `cancelGate` (`Completer`)
  and a `cancelCalls` log.
- **`OrderDetailCubit`:** `Future<CancelOutcome> cancel(CancelReason? reason)`.
  - It emits `OrderDetailLoaded` with `cancelling: true` while the request runs.
  - On success it emits the returned order.
  - It returns `CancelOutcome.{cancelled, cutoffPassed, notCancellable, failed}`. The screen
    maps the outcome to FR-3's UI.
- **`ScheduledDeliveryCubit`:** `Future<SkipOutcome> cancelDelivery()`, returning
  `{cancelled, cutoffPassed, failed}`.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

| Area | Target |
|------|--------|
| Correctness | The app never shows "Refunded" while `refundState == PENDING`; never shows "not charged" for a customer cancel; never offers cancel past `cancellableUntil` |
| Accessibility | Every new button ≥ 48 dp; reason chips announce selected state; a busy confirm button keeps the label "Cancel order" via `Semantics` (same pattern as MA-152 Reorder); destructive actions use the error colour **plus** explicit wording (never colour alone) |
| Performance | One request per cancel; the screen redraws from the response (no extra GET) |
| Resilience | A network failure keeps the sheet and the reason; a double tap is impossible (button disabled while in flight) |
| Maintainability | Copy lives in `order_status_copy.dart` and a new `cancel_copy.dart` (domain), not inline in widgets |

## 7. Data Considerations

- No local persistence.
- All new fields are nullable and parsed tolerantly. An older backend, before MA-154 is
  deployed, returns none of them, and then the Cancel action simply never shows. That's safe.

## 8. Integration Considerations

| Dependency | Contract | Failure behaviour |
|------------|----------|-------------------|
| Order Service `POST /orders/{id}/cancel` (MA-154) | FR-4 response; `CUTOFF_PASSED`, `ORDER_NOT_CANCELLABLE` | FR-3 mapping |
| Order Service DTO fields (MA-154 FR-7) | `cancellableUntil`, `refundState`, … | Missing → no Cancel action (safe default) |
| Subscription Service `POST /subscriptions/{id}/skip` (existing) | `CUTOFF_PASSED`, `DATE_NOT_DUE` | FR-5 mapping; `DATE_NOT_DUE` → the generic error |
| Wallet (MA-153) | Indirect: the refund appears in Transaction History as "Refund for Order" (MA-149) | — |

**Deploy order:** MA-153 → MA-154 → this app release. The app degrades safely if released
earlier (§7).

## 9. Edge Cases and Failure Modes

| Case | Behaviour |
|------|-----------|
| Screen left open across the cut-off | The Cancel action stays visible until the next rebuild. Tapping it then gets `409 CUTOFF_PASSED`, and FR-3 reloads and hides it. Acceptable: the server is authoritative |
| Device clock wrong | The decision uses the server's `cancellableUntil`. The clock only decides visibility, and the server rejects late cancels |
| Double tap on confirm | Disabled while in flight; MA-154 is idempotent anyway |
| Cancel succeeds but `refundState: PENDING` | "Refund in progress" copy; pull-to-refresh later shows "refunded" |
| Skip when the delivery has already become an order | `CUTOFF_PASSED` copy, then refresh → `becameOrder` → the order's own Cancel (if still eligible) |
| Order cancelled on another device | Pull-to-refresh shows the cancelled state; any later cancel is a `200` replay (MA-154) |
| ₹0 order | Policy "Nothing was charged…"; result "Order cancelled." |

## 10. Testing Strategy

**Unit:**
- `order_summary_test.dart`: the new fields parse, and unknown or missing values become null.
- `order_status_copy_test.dart`:
  - `isKnownNotCharged` is false for `CUSTOMER_CANCELLED` and true for `CUTOFF_PASSED`;
  - the three `reasonText` variants;
  - `billCaption`.
- `cancel_copy_test.dart`: the policy line formatting in IST, and the reason labels.

**Cubit** (`detail_cubits_test.dart`):
- `cancel(NOT_HOME)` → the fake got `('ord_1', NOT_HOME)`, the state shows the returned
  cancelled order, and the outcome is `cancelled`.
- `cancelling` is true while gated, and a second `cancel()` while in flight is a no-op.
- `CUTOFF_PASSED` → the outcome `cutoffPassed` and the order reloaded.
  `ORDER_NOT_CANCELLABLE` → `notCancellable`.
- A network error → `failed`, and the state is unchanged.
- `ScheduledDeliveryCubit.cancelDelivery()` → `skip(subId, date)` called; success / cut-off /
  failure outcomes.

**Widget** (`detail_screens_test.dart`, fixed clock and tall viewport). Each scenario lists its
keys and the visible text it asserts.

- **Scenario: cancel a paid order.**
  - Given: a `CONFIRMED` order, amount ₹155, `cancellableUntil` = tomorrow-minus-one 20:00 IST
    (in the future), and the fake returning `CANCELLED`/`REFUNDED`.
  - Steps:
    1. Expect "Free cancellation until 8:00 PM, …".
    2. Tap `orderDetail.cancel`. Expect "Cancel this order?" and "You'll get a full refund of
       ₹155.00 to your Milkful Wallet."
    3. Tap `cancelOrder.reason.NOT_HOME`, then `cancelOrder.confirm`.
  - Outcome:
    - the SnackBar "Order cancelled. ₹155.00 refunded to your Wallet." with **Shop for
      tomorrow**;
    - the banner shows Cancelled;
    - the reason text says "You cancelled this order. ₹155.00 was refunded to your Wallet.";
    - the amount is not struck through;
    - tapping **Shop for tomorrow** lands on `/catalog`.
- **Scenario: keep order.** Tap cancel, then `cancelOrder.keep` → the sheet closes and the fake
  got no call.
- **Scenario: refund pending.** The fake returns `PENDING` → "Order cancelled. Your refund is
  on its way."; the reason text says "Your refund is in progress."
- **Scenario: cut-off passed on submit.** The fake throws `CUTOFF_PASSED` → the past-cut-off
  SnackBar, the Cancel action is gone, and "Cancellation closed at 8 PM the day before
  delivery." shows.
- **Scenario: network error.** The sheet stays, "Couldn't cancel. Try again." shows, and the
  selected reason chip is still selected.
- **Scenario: not offered.**
  - `cancellableUntil` null → no `orderDetail.cancel`, and the closed line shows.
  - The order is already cancelled, or `PAYMENT_FAILED` → no cancel UI at all.
- **Scenario: cancel a scheduled delivery.**
  - Given: before the cut-off.
  - Steps: tap `scheduled.cancel`, expect "Cancel this delivery?", tap
    `cancelDelivery.confirm`.
  - Outcome: the fake subscription repo got `skip('sub_1', date)`, the SnackBar "Delivery on …
    cancelled." shows, and the screen pops.
- **Scenario: scheduled delivery past the cut-off.** No `scheduled.cancel`; the closed line
  shows.

**Manual (local-dev):**
- Cancel a real checkout order → Transaction History shows "Refund for Order", and the
  balance is restored.
- Cancel tomorrow's subscription delivery → it's gone from My Orders.

## 11. Risks and Trade-offs

- **No mock for the cancel flow.** The patterns are borrowed from the existing Skip dialog and
  MA-152's actions row. Product should review the sheet's copy and layout.
- **Visibility of "Cancel delivery" uses the device clock** (the subscription DTO has no
  `cancellableUntil`). The server rejects late skips, so the worst case is a visible button
  that answers "past the cut-off". Adding a server-side field to Subscription Service was
  judged out of scope.
- **Cancel isn't on My Orders list rows.** This keeps destructive actions on screens that show
  the full order. It may be revisited after usage data comes in.
